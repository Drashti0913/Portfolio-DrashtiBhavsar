# Photos for Portfolio

Please add your photos to this directory with the following names:

## Required Photos

1. **hero-photo.jpg** - For the Hero section
   - Recommended: Your Northeastern University photo (the one with you standing in front of the university building)
   - Size: Ideally 800x800px or larger (square format works best)
   - Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP

2. **about-photo.jpg** - For the About section
   - Recommended: Your presentation photo (the one showing you presenting) or a professional headshot
   - Size: Ideally 600x800px or larger (portrait format)
   - Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP

3. **skills-photo-1.jpg** - For the Skills section (left side)
   - Recommended: One of your personal photos (e.g., from New York, Northeastern University, or presentation)
   - Size: Ideally 400x600px or larger (portrait format)
   - Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP

4. **skills-photo-2.jpg** - For the Skills section (right side)
   - Recommended: Another one of your personal photos (e.g., from New York, Northeastern University, or presentation)
   - Size: Ideally 400x600px or larger (portrait format)
   - Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP

5. **projects-header.jpg** - For the Projects section header (background image)
   - Recommended: A photo of you working on projects, coding, or at a desk
   - Size: Ideally 1920x400px or larger (wide format)
   - Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP

## How to Add Photos

1. Save your photos with the exact filenames above
2. Place them in this `public/images/` directory
3. The website will automatically use them

## Alternative: Using External URLs

If you prefer to host photos elsewhere, you can update the image sources in:
- `components/Hero.tsx` - Change the `src` attribute
- `components/About.tsx` - Change the `src` attribute
- `components/Skills.tsx` - Change the `src` attributes
